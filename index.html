What changed in October 2026

Show

Same system, same data — it just looks cleaner and the buttons are easier to find. Here is where things are now.

  • Menu. The everyday screens are at the top. Ops Dashboard, Error Log, Settings and Guides are under System. On a phone, tap ☰ at the top left to open the menu.
  • Help. Every screen has a Help button at the top right. Press it to see what the screen and each button do.
  • Strike Queue. Tasks are in three groups: P1 · Act today, P2 · This week, P3 · When you can. Click a task on the left. The right side shows What to do and who to call. Then use Start call, Open case file, Resolve or Snooze to (the next weekday, e.g. Snooze to Thu). The tabs All tasks, Mine and Overdue filter the list.
  • Snooze. Moves the task's due date to the next weekday. Nobody is contacted. The daily overdue email to the office also waits until that day.
  • AR Aging. The cards at the top are All invoices, 0–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days and 90+ days. Click one to see only those invoices. At the end of each row, the ☎ phone button logs a call. The › arrow opens the job's Actions tab.
  • Money and dates. A balance in green that says credit means more was paid than invoiced. Dates without a time no longer show a made-up time.

Words you will see

Show
AR
Accounts receivable — money customers and carriers still owe us.
DSO
Days sales outstanding — on average, how many days it takes to get paid. Lower is better.
Aging bucket
How old an unpaid invoice is: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 or 90+ days. The older, the harder to collect.
P1 · Act today
Most urgent task. Do these first.
P2 · This week
Important, but can wait a few days.
P3 · When you can
Low urgency.
Picked up
Someone opened the call card for this task, so it is being worked on.
Due date
The day a task should be done by. Shown in red when it has passed.
Snooze to …
Button in Strike Queue, e.g. Snooze to Thu. Moves the task's due date to the next weekday. The task stays on your list.
Credit
Shown in green: more was paid than invoiced. Check the job before chasing it.
Carrier
The insurance company that pays the claim.
Adjuster
The person at the carrier who handles the claim. Usually the one we call or email.
Diversion
The carrier sent the payment to the homeowner instead of to us.
Lien
Our legal right to claim payment on the property. It expires after a set number of days.
Case File
Everything about one job in one place. Tabs: Timeline, Invoices, Payments, Disputes, Contacts and Actions.
Guided view
A simpler layout some team members use: fewer columns and a What should I do? button.
